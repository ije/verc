Verc uses libVLC's public C API and VLC's filter interfaces. The
standalone build includes selected VLC 3.x libraries and playback plugins.
VLC and libVLC retain their own licenses and copyrights. Build-only plugin
headers are from VLC 3.0.24. Their LGPL license is in Vendor/VLC/COPYING.LIB,
and the app includes it as VLC-COPYING.LIB.txt.

Verc bundles libraries and plugins from the official VLC 3.0.24 macOS ARM64
build (vlc-3.0.24-arm64.dmg, SHA-256
64a89d93cdd30b0e97131743e246373db82d6826ea882d265d46d74b136da2b7).
Each Verc release publishes the VLC source next to its DMG:
vlc-3.0.24.tar.xz, SHA-256
e7cab503d1d7d5849b89d2cf0e1ee60d0ef6d012407791b644b9cfc0cc225fdf.
Releases: https://github.com/ije/verc/releases

Written offer: for at least three years after we last distribute a Verc
release, we will give any third party the complete corresponding source code of
the VLC libraries and plugins in that release, including the third-party
libraries they contain, for no more than our cost of physically performing
source distribution. Ask at https://github.com/ije/verc/issues.

If you distribute your own build, you must meet these duties yourself. Some VLC
configurations contain GPL v3 components and are not suitable for a GPL v2-only
distribution. Check the licenses of the plugins in scripts/vlc-plugins.json.

VLC source: https://code.videolan.org/videolan/vlc
VLC releases: https://download.videolan.org/pub/videolan/vlc/
